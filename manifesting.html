I Tricked the Universe Into Giving Me an iPhone 16 pro. Here's the Weird Smelly Secret That Worked

Maksim Felix
April 30, 2025
Manifestation Law Of Attraction

What’s the Law of Attraction (and Why Should You Care)? Let’s start simple: the Law of Attraction (LOA) is the idea that your thoughts shape your reality. Focus on positivity, and you’ll attract positive outcomes. Focus on lack? Well… you get the picture. It’s not magic—it’s about aligning your energy with what you want. And guess what? You can use it to manifest anything, even that sleek iPhone 16 Pro sitting on your vision board.

Step 1: Get Super Specific

First things first: no vague wishes! The universe loves details. Don’t just say, “I want an iPhone.” Say, “I want the iPhone 16 Pro in Gray, 256GB, with that matte finish I’ve been drooling over.” Picture it. Write it down. Screenshot it. The more precise, the better.

If it can be imagined, it can be moved into your reality.

Maksim Felix

Step 2: Fuel Your Vision with “Visual Proof”

Time to dive into the aesthetic rabbit hole. Scroll through photos of the iPhone 16 Pro. Watch unboxing videos on YouTube.

Notice the way light hits its titanium edges, the sound of peeling off the protective film, the weight of it in someone’s hand. Immerse your senses. This isn’t daydreaming—it’s training your brain to recognize your desire as real.

Step 3: Ignore the “But I’m Broke” Mindset

So your bank account currently reads “3.64 bob” (we’ve all been there 😅). Stop. Right. There. Limiting thoughts like “I can’t afford this” are kryptonite to the LOA.

Instead, think: “Money flows to me easily,” or “Opportunities for abundance are everywhere.” Your wallet’s current state? Irrelevant. Focus on the feeling of already having the phone.

Step 4: Create Your Mental Movie (and Star in It!)

Find a quiet spot, close your eyes, and hit “play” on this scene:

- You’re unboxing the iPhone 16 Pro. Feel the crisp packaging, smell that new-electronics scent (you know the one!), hear the snap of the box opening.

- You’re holding it. Turn it on. Swipe through apps. Text a friend: “Guess what I just got?!” Key detail: Use present tense in your mind. “I am unboxing it,” not “I will unbox it.”

Step 5: No Emotion = No iPhone 16pro

As you visualize, let yourself feel JOY. Giggles, goosebumps, happy tears—whatever works!

This emotional high is your “vibration” aligning with your desire. The happier you feel, the faster the universe responds.

(Pro tip: If imagining a new phone doesn’t spark joy, ask yourself why you want it. Is it confidence? Status? Dig deeper!).

The feeling is like the engine of a car. If it is missing, then you are, well, "fantasizing". The chances of your desire manifesting are close to zero...

Step 6: Repeat, Repeat, Repeat

Do this visualization daily—even for just 5 minutes.

Consistency convinces your subconscious that this iPhone is yours. Think of it like watering a plant: skip days, and it wilts. Stick with it, and voilà—growth!

Step 7: Take Inspired Action

Keep living your life, but stay open to opportunities. Maybe you’ll stumble on a side hustle, a surprise bonus, or a friend upgrading their phone.

Act on those nudges! The LOA isn’t about sitting on the couch—it’s about co-creating with the universe.

Step 8: Let the Universe Work Its Magic

Relax. The “how” isn’t your job. Your role is to stay positive, grateful, and ready to receive.

Trust that the universe has your back. (This isn’t giving up—it’s surrendering doubt!)

Step 9: The Art of Letting Go (Yes, Seriously)

Here’s the tricky part: stop obsessing. Once you’ve “programmed” your mind, release the need to control how or when the phone arrives.

In fact forget that you ever desired that iPhone 16pro. Don’t expect anything. It is called "Law of detachment" .

"Wait, Is This Real?!” Moment

One day, you’ll glance at your hand and—surprise!—there it is. Maybe you’ll buy it yourself, win it, or receive it as a gift. However it arrives, celebrate it like the cosmic win it is.

Bonus Tip: Want to Dive Deeper?

If you’re thinking, “But how does thought-energy actually work?!” grab the book “The Secret Power of the Universe” by Jenny Loveless. It breaks down the science (yes, science!) behind manifesting. Download it for free in our PowerCentre.

Final Thought

Manifesting isn’t just about iPhones—it’s about realizing your power to shape your life. Whether it’s a gadget, a relationship, or a dream job, if it can be imagined, it can be moved into your reality.
