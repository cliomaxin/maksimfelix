About Maksim

As a WordPress developer since 2009, I’ve had the pleasure of working with large corporations, universities, advertising agencies, publishers, law firms, and non-profit organizations.

In 2020 I co-founded CultivateWP, a WordPress agency focusing on high performance sites for web publishers.

I’ve contributed to WordPress core by fixing bugs and helping build new features. I was also a contributing developer to the Genesis theme framework.

I have been so fortunate to build a career on top of WordPress. I’ve contributed what I can to help the WordPress community that has helped me so much.

I share WordPress tutorials on my blog, my free WordPress plugins, and through code snippets. I’ve presented at many WordPress conferences and community events.

I’m also the co-founder of the BIL Conference, the free and open unconference that complimented the TED Conference.

I’m a loving husband and girl dad who calls Texas home. I love to learn and grow, and I often do this through my clients. I have dug deep into photography, gardening, whiskey collecting, ranching, and cooking.

My family and I enjoy long summer road trips. We have recently purchased a popup camper to take our love of camping and the outdoors to the next level. If we happen to work together in the summer months, you may find me working from a state or national park.
