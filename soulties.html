Soul Ties: The Invisible Bonds That Shape Your Life (And How to Break Free)

Maksim Felix
June 02, 2025
Spiritual Soul Ties

We’ve all had that one relationship—whether romantic, platonic, or even familial—that left a deep imprint on us. Long after it ended, we still feel their presence, their energy, even their emotions as if they never really left. This isn’t just nostalgia or lingering feelings; it’s something deeper—a soul tie.

Soul ties are energetic bonds that form between two people, connecting them on emotional, spiritual, and sometimes even physical levels. Some ties are beautiful, like those with a lifelong best friend or a loving partner. Others, however, can be toxic, draining, and keep us stuck in cycles of pain.

If you’ve ever wondered why you can’t seem to move on from someone, why you still feel their energy, or why you keep attracting the same kind of relationships, you feel emotionally drained, this blog is for you.

We’ll explore:

  1. What soul ties really are
  2. How they form (both healthy and unhealthy)
  3. How energy transfers between people through them
  4. How they directly impact your life (with real-life examples)
  5. How to break unhealthy soul ties
  6. How to Break Ties When You Don’t Remember Names
  7. The healing process (because you deserve freedom)
    8.

    (1) What Are Soul Ties?

    A soul tie is an invisible, energetic cord that connects you to another person. Think of it like a phone and Wi-Fi signal—even when a Wi-Fi router isn’t physically visible, the Wi-Fi network can still be detected by the phone.

    (2) How Soul Ties Are Formed

    These bonds are created through:

    • Physical intimacy (sex creates one of the strongest soul ties)
    • Deep emotional connections (love, friendship, trauma bonding)
    • Shared experiences (going through hardships together)
    • Manipulation or control (toxic relationships, narcissistic abuse)

    Not all soul ties are bad. A healthy soul tie might be the unshakable bond between a mother and child or two friends who’ve supported each other for decades. But unhealthy soul ties keep you energetically chained to someone who no longer serves you—or worse, harms you. In this article, we are going to address Soul Ties formed as a result of any form of Sexual acts.

    (3) How Soul Ties Form (And How Energy Transfers Through Them)

    1. Physical Intimacy

    Sex is one of the fastest ways to form a soul tie. In many spiritual traditions, intercourse is seen as an exchange of energy—two souls merging, even temporarily. This is why casual sex can sometimes leave people feeling emotionally attached or drained without understanding why.

    Example 1: Jake had a one-night stand but couldn’t stop thinking about the woman afterward. He felt inexplicably connected to her, even though they barely knew each other.

    Example 2: The same woman who had sex with Jake above (Let’s say her name is Cate), she had sex with 4 more men before him, Jake is now attached to 4 more spirits. In total, Jake is in a web of 5 soul ties. And now try to think of the 4 men that Cate slept with and how many soul ties they were pre-linked to before Cate happened… That is how complex soul ties can go from just one sexual encounter. Jack is having of god knows how big the web or chain could be; just because of that one night stand.

    2. Emotional Vulnerability

    When you open your heart to someone—sharing secrets, fears, dreams—you create an energetic bridge. This is why breakups with deep emotional connections hurt so much; you’re not just losing the person, you’re Breaking an energy link.

    Example: Sarah and Mark dated for five years. Even after their breakup, Sarah would randomly feel Mark’s sadness or anger, as if his emotions were her own. This wasn’t coincidence—it was a lingering soul tie.

    3. Trauma Bonding

    Going through intense experiences together (good or bad) can create soul ties. Soldiers who survive war together, survivors of abuse, or even two people who partied hard in their youth may remain energetically linked long after they’ve moved on.

    Example: Lisa and her ex went through a toxic, on-again-off-again relationship. Even years later, whenever Lisa tried to date someone new, she’d feel her ex’s jealousy—like an invisible hand pulling her back.

    (5) How To: Breaking Soul Ties the Right Way

    Soul ties are not just emotional or psychological—they are spiritual bonds that require spiritual solutions. Just as they were formed in the unseen realm, they must be broken in the same way. And in the spiritual world, blood holds supreme power.

    Why Blood Matters in the Spiritual Realm

    Blood is the essence of life. In ancient and spiritual traditions, blood represents covenants, sacrifices, and binding agreements. The highest form of blood is that of a human being, because human life carries the greatest spiritual weight.

    The blood cleanses all unrighteousness—not just the ones you can name (1st John 1:7).

    When the God offered His Son as a sacrifice, it was not just an act of love—it was the ultimate spiritual transaction. The blood that was shed holds the power to break every curse, every bondage, and every ungodly soul tie. No other force in heaven or earth is stronger.

    This means:

    1. Human blood has authority in the spirit realm.
    2. The blood of the Son of God is the most powerful cleansing and cutting force.
    3. Faith in this blood is what activates its power in your life.
    4. If you want to be truly free from unhealthy soul ties, you must approach it spiritually—with faith, declaration, and the authority of the blood.

    Declare:
    "By the blood of the Son of God, every demonic record of my past connections is destroyed. Every claim the enemy had over me through these ties is nullified. I am free!"

    Step 4: Seal Your Freedom with Faith

    This is not magic—it operates by faith. You may not feel different immediately, but if you believe in the power of the blood, the spiritual realm must respond.

    Declare: "I walk in my freedom now. No soul tie can reattach because the blood of the Son of God has spoken. My spirit is clean, my mind is clear, and my future is untied from my past. Amen."

    The enemy(Satan) cannot override the blood. It is the highest spiritual authority. Faith activates the power—you must believe the ties are broken.

    Avoid Re-engagement – Do not reconnect with these people unless absolutely necessary (and even then, guard your spirit). Guard Your Gates – Be mindful of future relationships. Not every connection should have access to your spirit. Renew Your Mind – Replace old memories with truth. You are no longer tied.

You will step into new levels of freedom. The enemy wants you to believe these ties are unbreakable—but the blood says otherwise. Walk in your deliverance. "The blood that was shed has broken every chain. You are free indeed."

    (6) Breaking Soul Ties When You Can’t Remember Every Name (Healing for High "Body Counts")

    Some soul ties are formed so casually—through hookups, one-night stands, or reckless seasons—that you can’t even recall every name. But the spiritual records remain. The enemy doesn’t need you to remember for the ties to affect you. The good news? The blood of the Son of God doesn’t require perfect recall to break every chain. Here’s how to Break these ties when you can’t name them individually.

    Why This Still Works (Spiritual Principles)

    The Blood Covers What You Don’t Remember.
    God’s power isn’t limited by human memory.
    Group Ties Still Have a Collective Stronghold.
    Multiple sexual partners create a web of soul ties that drain energy, attract chaos, and block destiny.
    You don’t need to name each spider to break the whole web.
    Faith Overrides Forgetfulness
    It’s not about the precision of your words but the power of your faith in the blood that was shed by the Messiah during the crucifixion.

    Trust that the universe has your back. (This isn’t giving up—it’s surrendering doubt!)

    How to Break Ties When You Don’t Remember Names

    Step 1: Repent & Surrender

    Before cutting ties, cleanse your conscience. The blood can’t flow where there’s unrepentance.

    "Heavenly Father, I repent for every sexual connection outside of Your will—known and unknown. Forgive me for giving pieces of my soul away carelessly. I surrender every part of my past to You. Wash me clean by the blood of Your Son."

    Step 2: Break All Unnamed Soul Ties

    Since you can’t name them individually, address them collectively under the blood:
    "In the name of the Most High, I Break every soul tie formed through sexual intimacy with anyone whose name I can’t remember. By the power of the blood that was shed, I cancel every covenant, every energy link, and every demonic claim attached to these encounters. My body, soul, and spirit are no longer tied to these people. The blood of the Son of God dissolves every connection now!"

    After the Cutting: Staying Free

    Stop Reinforcing relationships.
    No more revisiting old flings (even in your mind).
    Avoid porn or lustful fantasies—they reactivate old ties.
    Guard Your Temple and Body.
    Your body is sacred. Treat future intimacy with wisdom.
    Renew Your Mind. Start incorporating new habits such as reading self help books if you can.
    Replace shame with truth: "The blood has made me clean. I am not defined by my past."

    Encouragement

    You don’t have to carry the weight of every mistake. The blood doesn’t ask for a full list—it asks for faith.
    Whether it was 5 people or 50, the same power breaks every chain. Walk in your freedom—no guilt, no shame, no lingering ties.

    "The blood doesn’t need a name to break a chain."

    Healing isn’t instant. You might still feel their energy for a while—that’s normal. But with time, you’ll notice:

    • Less emotional weight when you think of them.
    • More mental clarity and peace.

    Breaking a soul tie isn’t about erasing the past; it’s about reclaiming your future. You deserve relationships that uplift you, not ones that leave you feeling haunted by ghosts of connections past.
